Xenosaga Episode I — Story Mode

Close PCSX2. Drag the patches folder onto your PCSX2 folder, into the same place as the patches folder that is already there. Replace the file when asked.

The PCSX2 folder is the one Settings, Folders calls the Patches directory.

Linux, including this machine's AppImage:
~/.config/PCSX2/

Windows:
Documents/PCSX2/

Flatpak:
~/.var/app/net.pcsx2.PCSX2/config/PCSX2/

Start the game. Story Mode is on every time. There is no cheat box to tick, and it is still on after you quit. Enable Patches is the default and must stay on.

Your hits deal 3x damage. You take 1/4 damage. Healing and EP costs stay the same. A hit still does at least 1. The floating number can still show the original damage. The HP bar is the one that changed.

To use Easy Mode instead (2x damage dealt, 1/2 damage taken), replace that same pnach file with optional/easy-mode/SLUS-20469_6D1276AB.pnach. Do not keep both.

To turn it off, delete SLUS-20469_6D1276AB.pnach from the patches folder.

USA disc only. Serial SLUS-20469, CRC 6D1276AB.
