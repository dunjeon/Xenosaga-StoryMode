Xenosaga — Story Mode

Close PCSX2. Copy the .pnach files from cheats/ into the PCSX2 cheats folder.

Linux, including this machine's AppImage:
~/.config/PCSX2/cheats/

Windows:
Documents/PCSX2/cheats/

Flatpak:
~/.var/app/net.pcsx2.PCSX2/config/PCSX2/cheats/

Right-click the game, open Properties, then Cheats. Turn on Enable Cheats there, and tick Story Mode or Easy Mode. Tick one.

Story Mode: you deal 3x damage and take 1/4.
Easy Mode: you deal 2x damage and take 1/2.

Healing and EP costs stay the same. A hit still does at least 1.

USA discs.

Episode I:                SLUS-20469, CRC 6D1276AB
Episode II disc 1:       SLUS-20892, CRC EB39ABEC
Episode II disc 2:       SLUS-21133, CRC EB39ABEC
Episode III disc 1:      SLUS-21389, CRC 2088950A
Episode III disc 2:      SLUS-21417, CRC 2088950A

Episode II and Episode III each use one cheat, saved under both disc serials. Booting a disc loads that disc's file. A disc swap during the game keeps the cheat that loaded with the boot disc.
