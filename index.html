Xenosaga Episode I — Story Mode

A free, open-source cheat for the USA PlayStation 2 game. Fan project. Not made by or affiliated with Monolith Soft, Namco, or Bandai Namco. Made with Grok by xAI. MIT license.

Download the latest release

Install

  1. Close PCSX2.
  2. Copy cheats/SLUS-20469_6D1276AB.pnach into the PCSX2 cheats folder.
  3. Right-click the game, open Properties, then Cheats. Turn on Enable Cheats for this game, and tick Story Mode or Easy Mode.
