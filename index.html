Xenosaga — Story Mode

A free, open-source cheat for the USA PlayStation 2 games Xenosaga Episode I, Episode II, and Episode III. Fan project. Not made by or affiliated with Monolith Soft, Namco, or Bandai Namco. Made with Grok by xAI. MIT license.

Download the latest release

Install

  1. Close PCSX2.
  2. Copy the .pnach files from cheats/ into the PCSX2 cheats folder.
  3. Right-click the game, open Properties, then Cheats. Turn on Enable Cheats for this game, and tick Story Mode or Easy Mode.
Linux, and the AppImage:  ~/.config/PCSX2/cheats/
Windows:                   Documents/PCSX2/cheats/

Episode II and Episode III each have two discs and one cheat. The two files for a game are the same code under each disc's serial. Booting a disc loads that file. A disc swap during the game keeps the cheat from the disc you booted.
